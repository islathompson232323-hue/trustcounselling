( 03 — How I work )

Time and space to talk openly.

01

Not telling you what to do

Counselling isn’t about me deciding what’s best for you. It’s about giving you room to talk about what’s happening and, together, understanding what might be keeping you stuck.

02

Person-Centred at the heart

Listening to you, understanding your experience and accepting you without judgement.

03

Integrative, where it helps

One approach doesn’t fit everyone, so I can also draw on elements of CBT and Transactional Analysis when they may be helpful.

( 04 — About me )

Trust isn’t just
the name above
the door.

I grew up in a fostering family, and later became a foster carer myself.

From an early age I saw how people’s experiences shape the way they see themselves, their relationships and the world around them. Fostering taught me the importance of patience, consistency and, perhaps most importantly, trust.

I’m a professionally trained counsellor and a registered member of the British Association for Counselling and Psychotherapy (BACP). I work within their Ethical Framework, with regular clinical supervision and continuing professional development.
